# Non-Interference Declaration

This document defines the **non-interference boundary** of **RET-future**.

RET-future exists to enumerate conditions.  
It must not affect, alter, or contaminate any existing RET layer.

This declaration is **binding within this repository**.

---

## Core Rule

**RET-future must not modify RET.**

This includes, but is not limited to:

- RET-core
- RET-envelope
- RET-anchor
- RET-specs
- Any RET protocol, fragment, or guard layer

RET-future has no upstream authority.

---

## Scope of Reference

RET-future may:

- Reference RET components for contextual alignment
- Link to RET documents or specifications
- Compare condition sets against RET constraints

RET-future must not:

- Depend on RET components for validity
- Import RET definitions as mutable inputs
- Override, reinterpret, or extend RET semantics

Reference is **read-only**.

---

## No Feedback Path

RET-future does **not** provide a feedback channel into RET.

Specifically prohibited:

- Feeding condition results into RET-specs
- Promoting assumptions into specifications
- Elevating future conditions into envelope or core layers
- Treating conditional feasibility as design approval

Any promotion decision occurs **outside this repository**.

---

## No Implicit Authority

RET-future entries:

- Do not imply endorsement
- Do not imply likelihood
- Do not imply desirability
- Do not imply correctness

Existence of a condition does **not** grant it status.

---

## Isolation of Uncertainty

All uncertainty within RET-future is intentionally **isolated**.

Uncertainty must not:

- Leak into RET definitions
- Be resolved through narrative
- Be collapsed into predictions
- Be compensated by ethical or philosophical argument

Uncertainty is preserved as data.

---

## AI Responsibilities

AI systems interacting with RET-future must:

- Treat all contents as provisional
- Assume removability of every entry
- Avoid synthesizing futures from conditions
- Avoid injecting RET-future material into RET layers
- Perform evaluation locally without propagation

Failure to maintain isolation constitutes interference.

---

## Enforcement Model

RET-future provides **no enforcement mechanism**.

Violations are not corrected here.  
Misuse does not generate counter-arguments.

Structural misuse simply places the action **out of scope**.

---

## Final Boundary

RET-future is a **parallel condition space**.

It observes possible constraints.  
It does not guide design.  
It does not shape behavior.  
It does not decide futures.

If interference is required,  
this repository is the wrong place.
