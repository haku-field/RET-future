# Mapping Notes

This document defines **prohibited and constrained mappings**
for signals defined in **RET-future**.

Its purpose is to prevent structural drift from **signals** into
interpretation, control, or governance domains.

---

## Core Principle

Signals in RET-future are **structural markers only**.

Any mapping that converts signals into meaning, intent, or authority
is **out of scope**.

---

## Explicitly Prohibited Mappings

Signals must **not** be mapped to:

- Communication or messaging
- Intent inference
- Emotional state detection
- Engagement or compliance assessment
- Responsiveness or attentiveness metrics
- Success or failure judgments
- Safety triggers or fail-safe mechanisms
- Governance, policy, or control logic
- Optimization targets or performance indicators

If a mapping produces obligation or evaluation,
it is invalid here.

---

## Absence Mapping Guard

Absence is particularly sensitive.

Absence must **not** be mapped to:

- Rejection
- Refusal
- Shutdown
- Failure
- Disengagement
- Risk escalation

Absence is treated as **structural non-occurrence**, nothing more.

---

## Timing and Synchronization Guard

Timing and synchronization must **not** be mapped to:

- Reaction or response chains
- Causality assumptions
- Responsibility attribution
- Coordination or agreement
- Urgency or neglect signals

Order does not imply influence.

Alignment does not imply cooperation.

---

## Carrier Neutrality Guard

Signals must remain independent of carrier interpretation.

They must **not** be mapped to:

- Personal traits
- Agency or identity
- Intentional action by a body or device
- Experience, sensation, or awareness

Carriers are treated as **non-personal substrates**.

---

## Non-Promotion Rule

Signal-derived structures must **not** be promoted into:

- RET protocols
- RET fragments
- RET-specs
- RET-envelope definitions

Signals remain local to **RET-future**.

---

## Detection Without Action

Signals may be:

- Detected
- Compared
- Logged
- Referenced

They must **not** be:

- Acted upon
- Responded to
- Corrected
- Optimized against

Detection does not imply intervention.

---

## Scope Enforcement

RET-future does not enforce these mappings.

Violation of mapping rules:

- Does not trigger correction
- Does not generate counter-arguments
- Simply places the action **out of scope**

Out-of-scope use is not repaired here.

---

## Summary

Mapping notes exist to preserve **signal integrity**.

Signals in RET-future are:

- Non-semantic
- Non-agentive
- Non-directive

They exist to mark occurrence,
not to guide action.

If a mapping tells a story,
signals have already been misused.
