# Unknowns

This document enumerates **unknown variables** within **RET-future**.

Unknowns are not gaps to be filled.  
They are **unbounded variables** whose properties, limits, or existence are not yet determined.

RET-future preserves unknowns without attempting resolution.

---

## Role of Unknowns

Unknowns exist to:

- Prevent premature closure
- Block speculative completion
- Separate ignorance from assumption
- Maintain falsifiability of condition sets

An unknown is **not** a problem to solve.

---

## Nature of Unknowns

All unknowns are:

- Unbounded
- Non-derivable
- Non-authoritative
- Resistant to inference

They may remain unknown indefinitely.

Presence does not imply future discovery.

---

## Allowed Unknown Types

RET-future unknowns may include:

- Existence of detectable signal channels
- Limits of synchronization across substrates
- Stability thresholds of carrier systems
- Emergent interference patterns
- Cross-layer compatibility behavior
- Long-horizon degradation effects
- Non-intervention observability limits

Each unknown must be stated **without projection**.

---

## Disallowed Treatments

Unknowns must **not** be:

- Estimated
- Ranked
- Assigned probability
- Framed as risks
- Converted into assumptions
- Rewritten as challenges or opportunities

Any attempt to bound an unknown without evidence is invalid here.

---

## Relationship to Assumptions

Unknowns differ from assumptions:

- Assumptions are *temporary premises*
- Unknowns are *unconstrained variables*

An assumption may be demoted to an unknown.  
An unknown must not be promoted to an assumption without explicit external grounding.

---

## Independence Rule

Each unknown must be:

- Independently removable
- Explicitly scoped
- Free of hidden dependency

Unknowns do not accumulate meaning through quantity.

---

## Failure and Unknowns

Unknowns do not cause failure by themselves.

Failure occurs only when:

- An assumption relying on an unknown collapses
- A constraint is violated regardless of unknown state

Unknowns remain inert until referenced elsewhere.

---

## Documentation Rule

Each unknown entry must include:

- A literal description of what is unknown
- The boundary of non-knowledge
- The affected evaluation scope, if any

Speculation is prohibited.

---

## Summary

Unknowns in RET-future are:

- Preserved
- Unresolved
- Unoptimized
- Non-directive

They mark the edges of knowledge,  
not the next steps beyond it.

If an unknown begins to suggest direction,  
it has exceeded its role and must be removed.
