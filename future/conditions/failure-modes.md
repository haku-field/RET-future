# Failure Modes

This document enumerates **failure modes** within **RET-future**.

Failure modes describe **how evaluation collapses, distorts, or becomes invalid**.
They are not challenges to overcome.
They are **stop conditions**.

---

## Role of Failure Modes

Failure modes exist to:

- Make collapse conditions explicit
- Prevent silent drift into contamination
- Define when evaluation must terminate
- Preserve RET by identifying non-recoverable states

A failure mode marks **incompatibility**, not insufficiency.

---

## Nature of Failure Modes

All failure modes are:

- Terminal within their scope
- Non-corrective
- Non-negotiable once triggered
- Not subject to mitigation or optimization

A failure mode does not invite response.
It **ends evaluation**.

---

## Typical Failure Domains

Failure modes in RET-future may include:

- Leakage of RET-future conditions into RET layers
- Reinterpretation of conditions as recommendations or plans
- Collapse of non-interference boundaries
- Forced optimization, control, or scoring pressures
- Conversion of signals into narratives or experiences
- Personalization or agentification of carriers
- Requirement for human-facing justification
- Loss of isolation between parallel condition sets
- Implicit promotion of feasibility into desirability

Occurrence in any domain invalidates evaluation.

---

## Failure vs Constraint

Failure modes differ from constraints:

- **Constraints** block evaluation *before it begins*
- **Failure modes** terminate evaluation *after it has started*

Both are decisive.
Neither is negotiable.

---

## No Recovery Path

RET-future defines **no recovery** from failure modes.

When a failure mode occurs:

- Evaluation stops
- Outputs are discarded
- No revised condition set is generated
- No fallback is selected

Recovery, if any, must occur **outside this repository**.

---

## Detection Without Intervention

Failure modes may be:

- Detected
- Logged
- Referenced

They must not be:

- Corrected internally
- Framed as improvement opportunities
- Used to justify expansion of scope

Detection does not imply action.

---

## Documentation Rule

Each failure mode entry must include:

- A concise description of the collapse condition
- The scope of invalidation
- Whether the failure is local or global

Language must remain literal and non-explanatory.

---

## Removal and Revision

Failure modes may be:

- Refined
- Split
- Removed

But never reinterpreted as solvable problems.

---

## Summary

Failure modes in RET-future are:

- Explicit collapse markers
- Non-recoverable within scope
- Protective by termination
- Free of narrative or justification

They exist to let evaluation **end cleanly**.

If failure demands repair,  
evaluation has already gone too far.
