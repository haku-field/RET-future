# Assumptions

This document enumerates **assumptions** used within **RET-future**.

Assumptions are **conditional premises**, not beliefs.  
They exist only to define *what must provisionally hold* for evaluation to occur.

---

## Role of Assumptions

Assumptions in RET-future serve to:

- Declare provisional premises explicitly
- Prevent hidden or implicit dependencies
- Allow evaluation attempts without commitment
- Enable clean invalidation when premises fail

An assumption is **not** a claim of truth.

---

## Nature of Assumptions

All assumptions are:

- Provisional
- Revocable
- Non-authoritative
- Replaceable

An assumption may be false, incomplete, or permanently invalid.

Its presence does not justify its correctness.

---

## Allowed Assumption Types

RET-future assumptions may concern:

- Physical or computational limits
- Signal detectability or synchronizability
- Stability of carrier substrates
- Persistence of relational constraints
- Compatibility boundaries between layers
- Observability without intervention
- Absence of forced optimization or control pressures

Each assumption must remain **testable in principle**.

---

## Disallowed Assumption Types

The following must **not** appear as assumptions:

- Assertions of inevitability
- Normative judgments
- Ethical justification
- Desirability or preference
- Human intention or consent framing
- Claims of future convergence

If an assumption implies *ought*, it is invalid here.

---

## Isolation Requirement

Each assumption must be written so that:

- It can be removed without breaking others
- Its failure does not cascade silently
- Dependencies are explicitly stated, if any

Implicit assumptions are considered **structural errors**.

---

## Failure Handling

If an assumption fails:

- The related condition set is invalidated
- No corrective action is taken
- No alternative assumption is auto-generated

Failure terminates evaluation quietly.

---

## Assumptions vs Unknowns

Assumptions differ from unknowns:

- Assumptions are **temporary premises**
- Unknowns are **unbounded variables**

An assumption may later be reclassified as an unknown or removed entirely.

---

## Documentation Rule

Each assumption entry must include:

- A concise statement
- The scope it affects
- Known limits or fragility, if applicable

Assumptions must remain **brief and literal**.

---

## Summary

Assumptions in RET-future are:

- Explicit
- Temporary
- Disposable

They enable evaluation without commitment.

When an assumption begins to stabilize into belief,  
it must be removed.
