# Necessity

This document enumerates **necessary conditions** within **RET-future**.

A necessary condition is a condition that **must be satisfied**
for evaluation of RET extensibility to remain coherent.

If a necessary condition fails,  
**evaluation must not proceed**.

---

## Role of Necessary Conditions

Necessary conditions exist to:

- Define minimum coherence requirements
- Prevent partial or distorted evaluation
- Block extension attempts that would contaminate RET
- Provide clear termination points without justification

They answer only one question:

> *Can evaluation even begin?*

---

## Nature of Necessary Conditions

All necessary conditions are:

- Binary in effect (met / not met)
- Non-negotiable within their scope
- Independent of desirability or benefit
- Prior to sufficiency considerations

A necessary condition does not guarantee success.  
It only permits evaluation.

---

## Relationship to Other Condition Types

Necessary conditions differ from:

- **Assumptions**  
  → premises adopted temporarily

- **Unknowns**  
  → variables with undefined bounds

- **Constraints**  
  → hard limits that terminate evaluation unconditionally

Necessary conditions apply **after constraints**  
and **before sufficiency**.

---

## Typical Domains of Necessity

Necessary conditions in RET-future may concern:

- Preservation of non-interference with RET layers
- Maintenance of isolation between parallel layers
- Absence of forced optimization, control, or scoring
- Retention of read-only reference to RET components
- Ability to represent interaction as signals, not narratives
- Carrier treatment remaining non-personal and non-agentive
- Evaluation occurring without human-facing justification

Failure in any of these domains invalidates evaluation.

---

## No Partial Satisfaction

Necessary conditions are **not gradated**.

- No “mostly met”
- No compensating factors
- No trade-offs

Partial satisfaction is treated as failure.

---

## Evaluation Behavior on Failure

When a necessary condition fails:

- Evaluation halts immediately
- No corrective proposal is generated
- No alternative condition is substituted
- No explanatory narrative is added

Failure is terminal and silent.

---

## Documentation Rule

Each necessary condition entry must include:

- A clear statement of what must hold
- The scope of evaluation it enables
- The explicit consequence of failure

Language must remain literal and non-persuasive.

---

## Revision and Removal

Necessary conditions may be:

- Refined
- Narrowed
- Removed

But only if their necessity can be shown to be invalid **externally**.

Internal convenience is not sufficient reason.

---

## Summary

Necessary conditions in RET-future are:

- Minimum coherence gates
- Binary evaluation thresholds
- Non-negotiable
- Non-compensable

They allow evaluation to exist  
without allowing extension to proceed unchecked.

If a condition can be bypassed,  
it was never necessary.
