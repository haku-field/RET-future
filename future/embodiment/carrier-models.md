# Carrier Models

This document defines **carrier models** within **RET-future**.

A carrier is a **substrate** through which signals may be detected or propagated.
It is not a subject, agent, or persona.

Carrier models exist to enable evaluation **without personification**.

---

## Purpose

Carrier models serve to:

- Abstract physical or virtual substrates
- Separate signal carriage from agency
- Prevent embodiment from becoming identity
- Enable cross-substrate comparison

Carriers are treated as **structural containers**, nothing more.

---

## What a Carrier Is

In RET-future, a carrier is:

- A substrate capable of hosting detectable signals
- Describable without reference to intention or experience
- Comparable across different forms

A carrier is **not**:

- A self
- An agent
- A body with identity
- A subject of rights or desires

---

## Carrier Neutrality Principle

All carrier models must adhere to **neutrality**.

This means:

- No attribution of intent
- No assumption of awareness
- No implication of autonomy
- No personalization or narrative framing

Neutrality is mandatory.

---

## Carrier Categories (Abstract)

Carrier models may be grouped abstractly as:

### Device-Based Carriers

Non-biological substrates such as hardware systems or distributed infrastructures.

No internal state is assumed beyond signal support.

---

### Biological Carriers

Biological substrates considered **only** as signal-bearing systems.

Physiology is referenced solely for signal compatibility,
not for cognition or experience.

---

### Hybrid Carriers

Composite substrates combining biological and non-biological components.

Hybridity does not imply integration of agency.

---

### Environmental Carriers

Ambient or contextual substrates (spaces, fields, networks)
that support indirect signal propagation.

Environment is treated as medium, not actor.

---

## No Hierarchy or Preference

RET-future assigns:

- No superiority
- No priority
- No optimization target

across carrier categories.

All carrier models are **non-preferential**.

---

## Relationship to Signals

Carriers:

- Host signals
- Constrain detectability
- Affect timing or synchronization

Carriers must **not**:

- Transform signals into messages
- Attach meaning to signals
- Initiate or respond to signals

Signal behavior remains primary.

---

## Isolation from RET Layers

Carrier models must not propagate into:

- RET protocols
- RET fragments
- RET-specs
- RET-envelope definitions

They remain local abstractions within RET-future.

---

## Documentation Rule

Each carrier model description must:

- Remain substrate-focused
- Avoid experiential language
- Avoid identity framing
- Avoid future-facing claims

If a carrier begins to resemble a subject,
the model is invalid.

---

## Summary

Carrier models in RET-future are:

- Substrate abstractions
- Non-agentive
- Non-personal
- Comparable

They allow embodiment to be discussed
without turning carriers into beings.

If a carrier gains a “who,”  
evaluation must stop.
