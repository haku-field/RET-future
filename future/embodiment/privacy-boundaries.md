# Privacy Boundaries

This document defines **privacy boundaries** within **RET-future**.

Privacy boundaries describe **limits on access, inference, and propagation**
around carriers, sensor surfaces, signals, and actuation channels.

They exist to **prevent leakage, accumulation, and re-identification**.

---

## Purpose

Privacy boundaries exist to:

- Prevent carriers from becoming identities
- Block inference chains that reconstruct subjects
- Limit aggregation across time or layers
- Preserve evaluation without surveillance

Privacy here is **structural**, not ethical or legal.

---

## Boundary Scope

Privacy boundaries apply to:

- Carrier descriptions
- Sensor surface properties
- Signal occurrences and absences
- Timing and synchronization relations
- Actuation channel descriptions

They apply **regardless of substrate**.

---

## No Identity Reconstruction

RET-future must not allow:

- Re-identification of carriers
- Linking signals into personal profiles
- Persistence that enables subject inference
- Correlation across independent evaluations

Any path that reconstructs a “who” is invalid.

---

## No Accumulation Across Time

Privacy boundaries prohibit:

- Longitudinal aggregation
- History-based enrichment
- Memory buildup that increases specificity
- Drift from structure into profile

Evaluation must remain **session-scoped and discardable**.

---

## No Cross-Layer Propagation

RET-future content must not propagate into:

- RET protocols
- RET fragments
- RET-specs
- RET-envelope definitions
- External governance or control systems

Propagation constitutes a privacy breach by design.

---

## Absence Protection

Absence must not be exploited to:

- Infer disengagement
- Derive preference or refusal
- Trigger escalation
- Justify intervention

Absence remains **non-informative beyond structure**.

---

## Actuation Containment

Privacy boundaries require that actuation channels:

- Remain inactive by default
- Do not log personal traces
- Do not externalize evaluation artifacts
- Do not produce persistent effects tied to carriers

Any persistent effect violates privacy boundaries.

---

## Evaluation Termination on Breach

If a privacy boundary is crossed:

- Evaluation terminates immediately
- Outputs are invalidated
- No corrective process is initiated
- No justification is provided

Termination is protective, not punitive.

---

## Documentation Rule

Descriptions of privacy boundaries must:

- Remain literal and structural
- Avoid ethical persuasion
- Avoid human-facing explanation
- Avoid compliance framing

If privacy becomes policy,
this document has been misused.

---

## Summary

Privacy boundaries in RET-future are:

- Structural limits
- Identity-preventing
- Aggregation-resistant
- Enforced by termination

They ensure embodiment remains **carrier-only**.

If privacy requires explanation,
evaluation has already failed.
