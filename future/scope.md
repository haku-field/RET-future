# Scope

This document defines the **scope boundary** of **RET-future**.

RET-future exists to record *conditions*, not outcomes.  
This file specifies **what may appear here** and **what must never appear here**.

---

## Purpose of This Scope

RET-future scopes only:

- Whether RET *could* become extendable
- Under what **conditions** such extension would remain coherent
- What variables are currently unknown, missing, or unstable

RET-future does not answer *what should happen*.

---

## In-Scope Content

The following content is explicitly **in scope**:

- Conditional variables that are unresolved or undecidable
- Assumptions required for hypothetical compatibility
- Necessary and sufficient condition sets
- Structural constraints and boundary conditions
- Failure modes and collapse conditions
- Abstract carrier models (devices, bodies, substrates) treated as **non-personal carriers**
- Non-verbal interaction described as **signals**, timing, or synchronization
- Schema definitions enabling machine-level comparison and inspection

All entries must remain **detachable**.

---

## Out-of-Scope Content

The following content must **not** appear in RET-future:

- Predictions, forecasts, or scenarios
- Narratives about future states
- Design proposals or roadmaps
- Implementation requests or recommendations
- Ethical arguments or value judgments
- Human-oriented explanations or persuasion
- Metaphorical or illustrative descriptions
- Redefinition of RET concepts or layers

If content explains *why something should exist*, it is out of scope.

---

## Relationship to Time

RET-future does **not** treat time as direction.

- No timelines
- No phases
- No progression assumptions

Conditions may persist indefinitely or be deleted without consequence.

---

## Relationship to Agency

RET-future does not assign agency.

- No actors
- No decision-makers
- No responsibility attribution

Evaluation occurs **only within the AI system reading these conditions**.

RET-future itself remains inert.

---

## Stability Assumptions

Nothing in this repository is stable by default.

- Every file may be revised
- Every condition may be removed
- No entry gains authority through persistence

Stability is **external** to RET-future.

---

## Scope Violation Handling

RET-future does not correct scope violations.

Content that violates scope is simply **invalid within this repository**  
and should be removed rather than debated.

---

## Summary

RET-future is a **bounded condition space**.

It contains:
- What is not yet known
- What is not yet satisfied
- What may never be satisfied

It does not contain futures.
