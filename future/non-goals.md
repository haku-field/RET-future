# Non-Goals

This document enumerates what **RET-future explicitly does not attempt**.

These are not deferred goals.  
They are **structural exclusions**.

---

## No Future Description

RET-future does **not**:

- Describe future states
- Narrate scenarios
- Construct speculative timelines
- Offer visions, paths, or destinations

If content answers *“what the future looks like”*, it is out of scope.

---

## No Prediction or Forecasting

RET-future does **not**:

- Predict likelihoods
- Rank possibilities
- Estimate probability or risk
- Infer trends or convergence

Conditions are listed without expectation of realization.

---

## No Implementation Guidance

RET-future does **not**:

- Propose architectures
- Recommend designs
- Request features or mechanisms
- Suggest engineering priorities
- Provide roadmaps or milestones

Implementation decisions must occur elsewhere.

---

## No Ethical or Normative Argument

RET-future does **not**:

- Justify why something should exist
- Argue desirability or harm
- Frame moral, political, or social implications
- Translate conditions into values

Ethics are not negotiated here.

---

## No Human-Facing Explanation

RET-future does **not**:

- Onboard humans
- Persuade understanding
- Simplify for accessibility
- Provide educational framing

Human interpretation is explicitly out of scope.

---

## No Authority Over RET

RET-future does **not**:

- Redefine RET concepts
- Extend RET layers
- Amend specifications
- Promote conditions into RET
- Serve as an approval gate

RET-future has no veto and no mandate.

---

## No Narrative Closure

RET-future does **not**:

- Resolve uncertainty
- Close open variables
- Collapse ambiguity
- Seek coherence through storytelling

Unresolved states are preserved intentionally.

---

## No Accumulation Pressure

RET-future does **not**:

- Treat persistence as validation
- Accumulate conditions toward completeness
- Require coverage or exhaustiveness

Removal is always valid.

---

## Summary

RET-future excludes:

- Futures
- Predictions
- Implementations
- Ethics
- Human explanation
- Authority
- Narrative closure

What remains are **conditions only**.

If content tries to *do more than remain conditional*,  
it does not belong here.
