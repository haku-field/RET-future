# Misuse Patterns

The following distort RET-future into non-compliant forms.

## Roadmap laundering
Temporal contours are rewritten as implementation milestones.

Result:
future becomes pseudo-specification.

## Inevitability leak
Open contours are narrated as destiny, convergence, or certainty.

Result:
parallel condition collapses into future mythology.

## Migration projection
Discardable states are treated as required next steps.

Result:
non-authoritative status is lost.

## Unsatisfied variable overwrite
Open unknowns are prematurely replaced by fabricated closure.

Result:
future loses temporal openness.
