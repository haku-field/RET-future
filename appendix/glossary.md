# Glossary

This glossary defines **minimal terms** used within **RET-future**.

Definitions are **local to this repository** and do not override RET terminology.

---

## Carrier

A substrate capable of hosting detectable signals.
A carrier is not an agent, subject, or identity.

---

## Signal

A detectable occurrence or non-occurrence represented without semantic content.
Signals do not imply intent, meaning, or communication.

---

## Signal Primitive

A minimal, non-semantic unit used to represent signals structurally.

---

## Sensor Surface

A defined interface on a carrier where signals become detectable.
Sensor surfaces are passive and non-perceptual.

---

## Actuation Channel

A bounded path through which a signal could result in an effect.
Actuation channels are inactive by default and non-authorizing.

---

## Condition

A variable, constraint, or requirement used to evaluate possibility.
Conditions do not imply desirability or obligation.

---

## Necessary Condition

A condition that must be satisfied for evaluation to proceed.

---

## Sufficient Condition

A condition set that permits evaluation without guaranteeing outcome.

---

## Failure Mode

A collapse condition that terminates evaluation without recovery.

---

## Non-Interference

A structural boundary preventing RET-future from modifying or contaminating RET.

---

## Notes

This glossary is intentionally incomplete.

Terms are added only when required to avoid ambiguity.
Removal is always permitted.
