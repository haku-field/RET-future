# Glossary

## temporal contour
A non-binding time-shaped outline of unsatisfied variables.
Never a timeline.

## unsatisfied variable
A condition whose persistence across time remains open.
Satisfaction does not imply promotion.

## contour obsolescence
A state in which a temporal contour should be deleted
rather than preserved.

## duplication drift
Semantic reintroduction of equivalent contours
through renamed states or branches.

## inevitability leak
Any wording shift that makes recurrence appear destined.
